## Hi there!

I'm a Machine Learning & Autonomous Driving engineer based in Berlin. I am quite passionate about this space, particularly perception systems that are not just accurate, but reliably safe. My work sits at the intersection of 3D object detection, multi-object tracking, and trajectory prediction, with a focus on building pipelines that hold up in the real world, not just on benchmarks.


## About me

- Location: Berlin, Germany 🇩🇪
- Education: M.Sc. in Automation & Robotics
- Experience: ~2 years across ML, robotics & autonomous driving
- Expertise: 3D Object Detection · MOT · Trajectory Prediction · GNN · Transformers · Kalman Filters · Uncertainty Quantification · Sensor Fusion
- Currently Learning: Vision-Language Models (VLMs) · Vision-Language-Action Models (VLAs) · SLAM
